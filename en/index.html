VISION

Being the foundation of creativity,
and creating the next generation together.

Rootive aims to be the solid foundation that allows artists and creators to fully realize their creativity in their work.
However much the forms of entertainment change in this diverse and fast-moving world, the importance of the roots of making things, our original intent and the fundamentals, never changes.
From a position that knows the reality of the field, we provide support that truly matters.

The name comes from Roots (the fundamentals), Creative (creation) and Active (action).
Precisely because times change so quickly, we value the fundamentals and take on the world actively.
